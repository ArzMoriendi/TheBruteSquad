## Quick Summary

Write a short summary of who this NPC is and why they matter.

---



Small, 2–3 foot tall reptilian humanoids with scaly skin (ranging from red to rusty brown), small horns, and long tails. They are weak individuals known for being clever trap-builders, tunneling, pack tactics, and serving dragons

The Kobolds of the Emberforge Outpost are:

* Qarux
* Ding
* Areh
* Egga
* Scil
* Kud
* Vend
* Shrim



Egga explains that Qarux was banished from the overall kobold society in the Neverwinter Woods due to his inventions and eccentricity. However, the kobold elders did not want to lose sight of what he was working on, so they assigned the security detail led by Ding and support from Egga as a den mother to ensure Qarux’s safety and support.
