
 Mistress of the Night; Lady of Loss; Nightbringer; Dark Goddess; Lady of Forgetfulness; Singer of Eternal Night; Mistress of All That Is Hidden 
 
* **Alignment**: Neutral Evil 

- **Portfolio**: Darkness, night, loss, forgetfulness, secrets, caverns, unrevealed places, pain, grief, nihilism, hidden things 
   
- **Domains**: Death, Trickery

- **Symbol**: A black disk encircled with a purple border, often representing a dark moon eclipsing light; Sometimes a Crescent Moon symbol often on a dark background.

- Shar is one of the oldest deities of Toril, twin sister and eternal rival of Selûne. Together they emerged from primordial chaos, embodying darkness and light respectively
