Neverwinter lies on the Sword Coast North at the mouth of the Neverwinter River.  It is one of the larger cities in the region and known for crafts, trades, and cultural development. 

[[Boniface Abernathy]] has a headquarters there, in the docks region of the city.  Specifically, just west of Castle Neverember, after passing through the Blue Lake District.

[[Leyawiin]]'s bookstore is just outside the city proper in the hamlet of [[Primcross]].  This is Northeast of the tower district. 

Shops:
**Charter & Charm**:  Located in the Blue Lake District; specializes in enchantments. Operated by Lysandra

Locations:
Neverdeath Graveyard: final resting place for the dead of Neverwinter.  South of Castle Neverember.  Location of a temple to [[Kelemvor]]

NPCs:
Edran - a mysterious, elderly man in the Blue Lake district who knows [[Bronn the Humble|Bronn]]'s parents.
