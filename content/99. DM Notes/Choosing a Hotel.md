# Scene Three

# A Last Night Ashore

## DM Notes

This scene serves three purposes:

- It gives the party an opportunity to roleplay, rest, and prepare before committing to the voyage.
    
- It reinforces that Neverwinter is safe, civilized, and comfortable—the opposite of what awaits them.
    
- It provides the ideal transition into GhoulMan's vision.
    

Unless the party specifically seeks out another establishment, Urgash recommends three inns within walking distance of the harbor. Each caters to different travelers, but all are respectable.

---

# Option One

# The Salted Gull

_"A bed is a bed, and the sea doesn't care what you paid for it."_

---

### Read Aloud

> The Salted Gull squats between two aging warehouses overlooking the inner harbor. Its weathered sign creaks constantly in the breeze, depicting a rather disgruntled gull clutching a fish nearly as large as itself.
> 
> Inside, the common room is loud, warm, and perpetually crowded. Dockworkers crowd long wooden tables while sailors swap exaggerated stories over overflowing tankards. Somewhere in the back, someone is attempting to play a fiddle with considerably more enthusiasm than talent.
> 
> The scent of roasted fish, spilled ale, damp rope, and sea salt fills the room.
> 
> No one here appears wealthy.
> 
> Everyone here appears content.

---

## Accommodations

- **Cost:** 8 sp per private room, 2 sp for a bunk in the common loft.
    
- Clean enough, though well-worn.
    
- Supper consists of fish stew, fresh bread, and dark ale.
    
- Noise continues well past midnight.
    

---

## Proprietor

**Marta Hensley**

Human woman, mid-60s.

Former sailor's wife.

Warm, practical, endlessly patient.

---

### Dialogue

> "If you're looking for luxury, you've walked into the wrong building."
> 
> _(She grins.)_
> 
> "If you're looking for a clean bed and a hot meal..."
> 
> "Welcome."

---

### Rumors

Characters who spend the evening here may overhear one rumor for every hour they linger. Roll a d6.

|d6|Rumor|
|:-:|---|
|1|A merchant vessel recently returned from Chult missing nearly half its crew—not from monsters, but disease.|
|2|Several pirates have been spotted farther south than usual.|
|3|Someone claims dinosaurs can be trained like horses.|
|4|A sailor insists he saw a dragon turtle surface near the Chultan coast.|
|5|A drunken deckhand swears Port Nyanzaru's markets sell anything imaginable.|
|6|An old mariner quietly mutters, "The jungle isn't what kills you. It's what follows you home."|

# Option Two

# The Mariner's Rest

_Recommended Choice_

---

### Read Aloud

> Lanterns burn warmly beneath polished oak beams as you step inside The Mariner's Rest. Unlike the dockside taverns nearby, conversation here remains comfortably subdued.
> 
> Merchants compare maps over glasses of wine while caravan masters quietly negotiate contracts in shaded booths. Travelers from distant lands dine together without raising their voices, the crackling fireplace softening the steady sound of rain tapping against the windows.
> 
> A young bard gently plucks the strings of a lute near the hearth, providing music that complements conversation rather than competing with it.
> 
> For the first time in many days...
> 
> ...everything feels peaceful.

---

## Accommodations

- **Cost:** 1 gp per room.
    
- Comfortable feather beds.
    
- Locking doors.
    
- Hot bath available for 2 sp.
    
- Excellent meals.
    

Each guest receives breakfast before departure.

---

## Proprietor

**Edric Mallen**

Human

Former caravan guard.

Calm.

Observant.

Never forgets a face.

---

### Dialogue

> "Travelers?"
> 
> _(He studies your equipment.)_
> 
> "...No."
> 
> _(Smiles knowingly.)_
> 
> "Explorers."

If they mention Chult:

He nods respectfully.

> "Then enjoy tonight."
> 
> "You'll appreciate remembering it."

---

### Evening Meal

The inn serves:

- Roasted herb chicken
    
- Buttered potatoes
    
- Seasonal vegetables
    
- Fresh baked bread
    
- Honey mead or Neverwinter lager
    

Players who choose to eat here recover comfortably from weeks of travel. Encourage roleplay over dinner.

# Option Three

# The Argent Anchor

_"Luxury fit for nobles, admirals, and those who believe they deserve both."_

---

### Read Aloud

> Marble floors gleam beneath crystal chandeliers as liveried attendants quietly relieve arriving guests of cloaks and travel packs.
> 
> Rich mahogany paneling lines the walls while paintings of famous naval victories overlook an elegant dining hall where nobles, wealthy merchants, and decorated officers dine beneath soft candlelight.
> 
> Large windows overlook Neverwinter Harbor, offering an uninterrupted view of dozens of ships gently rocking upon moonlit waters.
> 
> Everything here whispers refinement.
> 
> Even the silence seems expensive.

---

## Accommodations

- **Cost:** 5 gp per suite.
    
- Private bath.
    
- Fine dining.
    
- Balcony overlooking the harbor.
    
- Dedicated servant staff.
    

---

## Proprietor

**Lady Corinne Ashcombe**

Human Noble

Elegant.

Polite.

Perfect manners.

Perfect memory.

---

### Dialogue

> "Welcome to the Argent Anchor."
> 
> "May I assume your journey begins tomorrow?"
> 
> _(She smiles warmly.)_
> 
> "Then allow us the privilege of ensuring you begin it well."

---

# A Quiet Evening

Regardless of where the party stays, allow each player an opportunity to describe how their character spends the evening.

Suggested prompts include:

- Writing letters home.
    
- Sharpening weapons.
    
- Praying.
    
- Studying spellbooks.
    
- Enjoying one final drink.
    
- Looking out across the harbor.
    
- Reflecting on Hammerfall Run.
    
- Discussing hopes—or fears—for Chult.
    

This is an excellent opportunity for players to deepen relationships before the isolation of the jungle.

---

# Night Falls

### Read Aloud

> As the evening grows late, conversations gradually fade into the quiet rhythm of the harbor beyond your windows.
> 
> Outside, ships rock gently at their moorings while distant bells echo across dark waters. Somewhere, a watchman calls the changing of the hour before silence once again settles over Neverwinter.
> 
> The smell of rain hangs faintly in the air.
> 
> Tomorrow, the _Dawnmere_ will sail.
> 
> Tomorrow, the familiar world will begin to slip behind you.
> 
> For now...
> 
> ...there is only warmth.
> 
> A soft bed.
> 
> The distant sound of waves.
> 
> And sleep.

---

# Transition to Scene Four

As the players settle in for a long rest, pause for a moment. Look around the table and say:

> "As the night deepens, one among you does not dream as mortals do."

Turn to GhoulMan's player.

> "GhoulMan..."
> 
> "...this dream is yours alone."

---

## DM Commentary

This scene is intentionally low on mechanics and high on atmosphere. It gives the players a chance to breathe after the logistics of shopping and before the supernatural weight of the coming visions. By emphasizing comfort, warmth, and ordinary life, you create a sharp emotional contrast with GhoulMan's dream and, later, the relentless dangers of Chult. It also quietly signals that the characters are crossing a threshold: this is the last night in a safe bed before the campaign becomes a story of endurance, survival, and difficult choices.
