I love this direction. One thing I want to suggest before I write it:

**Don't actually run the arena as a full combat encounter.**

Run it cinematically.

Think of it like the opening of _Gladiator_ or a playable cutscene in a video game. GhoulMan isn't meant to win because of optimized tactics—he's meant to prove something about _who he is_. If you stop every few minutes to look up rules or stat blocks, you'll lose the mythic feel.

Instead, I would use **three waves**, allowing GhoulMan to use his actual abilities, but summarize enemy actions unless something dramatic happens. You can even let the other players watch silently. They'll be invested without sitting through a 90-minute solo combat.

## DM Notes

This dream is unlike ordinary sleep.

GhoulMan does not dream.

He is **taken**.

Time has no meaning here. Whether the vision lasts a heartbeat or an entire lifetime is impossible to know.

Neither intends to waste the opportunity.

This is an evaluation.
