
# Session 19 Prep: _Shadows on the Road to Chult_

## Session Themes

- **Legacies of the Past**
    
- **Truth vs. Faith**
    
- **The Call to Adventure**
    
- **Choosing the Future**
    

This session should feel strongly roleplay-driven. The party has finished the Hammerfall Run arc and now stands on the threshold of the campaign's next major chapter: Chult. The goal is to resolve the Primcross detour, deepen Bronn's personal story, and secure the party's commitment to Boniface Abernathy's expedition.

---

# Session Structure

|Scene|Estimated Time|Purpose|
|---|---|---|
|Morning in Primcross|15-20 min|Close Sylvie thread|
|Journey to Neverwinter|10 min|Transition|
|Arrival in Neverwinter|15-20 min|Establish city atmosphere|
|Bronn Encounter|30-45 min|Advance Bronn storyline|
|Blue Lake District Exploration|20-30 min|Shopping/social roleplay|
|Boniface Meeting|45-60 min|Sign Chult contract|
|Session Ending|5 min|Tease Chult|

# Opening Narrative

## Read Aloud

> _Our last two sessions found our heroes delving deep beneath Hammerfall Run, where ancient dwarven puzzles and forgotten tombs guarded secrets long buried beneath stone. Within the depths of the mine, the Brute Squad discovered a hidden ritual chamber where a Thayan mage sought to corrupt an ancient dwarven relic known as the Stoneheart Reliquary. Battling goliath undead, a surviving member of the Ironclaw clan, and powerful necromantic magic, the party ultimately drove the mage away, though not before Veldar was frozen solid by a devastating Cone of Cold. In the aftermath, the party recovered the Reliquary and rested amidst the silent ruins, each member touched in different ways by dreams, visions, and newfound revelations._
> 
> _Emerging from the mountain, the party survived a brutal blizzard and avalanche before returning triumphantly to Mirabar. There, Justiciar Geldryn Axebinder revealed the true significance of the Stoneheart Reliquary and rewarded the Brute Squad handsomely for their service. Veldar was fully exonerated, Leyawiin found a new mentor in Harbek Bronzewarden and embraced the path of Evocation, and the party earned honored status within the city._
> 
> _In the days that followed, our adventurers debated their future, sold the deteriorating head of Kragnoroth through Mirabar's shadowy underworld, acquired supplies and companions—including Bronn's aged but spirited ram, Geezer—and ultimately chose to continue south. Using the Harpers' portal network, the Brute Squad traveled to a hidden station outside Neverwinter before briefly aiding the small hamlet of Primcross, where unsettling events and the mysterious disappearance of a young woman named Sylvie hinted that darker forces may yet be at work along the Sword Coast._
> 
> _Now, with Primcross behind them and Neverwinter looming ahead, old legacies, hidden truths, and new opportunities await._

# Scene 6 — Boniface Abernathy

## Location

**Luminous Meridian Trade Company Headquarters**

Dock District.

Three-story stone-and-timber office overlooking Neverwinter Harbor.

---

## Read Aloud

> Near the waterfront stands a handsome three-story building bearing a polished brass sign:
> 
> **LUMINOUS MERIDIAN TRADE COMPANY**
> 
> Sailors, merchants, and adventurers move in and out with surprising regularity while dockworkers unload crates bearing the company's emblem.

---

# Boniface Personality

Public Face:

- Loud.
    
- Friendly.
    
- Charismatic.
    
- Generous.
    
- Excellent memory.
    

Private Reality:

- Extremely calculating.
    
- Opportunistic.
    
- Sees adventurers as investments.
    

The party should not uncover this yet.

---

# Greeting

> "The Brute Squad! Wonderful!"
> 
> "Mirabar didn't kill you after all!"
> 
> "Come in, come in! Anyone who survives mountain necromancers deserves a proper drink."

---

# Roleplay Notes

Boniface should:

- Remember names.
    
- Compliment everyone.
    
- Laugh often.
    
- Offer quality food and drink.
    
- Speak in terms of opportunity.
    

Examples:

### To Leyawiin

> "A wizard who survived ancient dwarven magic? Chult adores clever people."

### To Veldar

> "A goliath of your stature is worth three mercenaries."

### To Bronn

> "Clerics keep expeditions alive."

---

# Chult Contract

Terms include:

- Company-funded transportation.
    
- Food and lodging aboard ship.
    
- Treasure division.
    
- Expedition expectations.
    
- Liability waivers.

Contract appears legitimate.

Additional information regarding Chult.
