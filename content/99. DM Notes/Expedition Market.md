# Scene Two

# The Expedition Market

## Overview

Following their conversation with Dockmaster Urgash Stonewake, the Brute Squad has one final opportunity to prepare for life beyond civilization. The expedition district bordering Neverwinter's harbor exists for one purpose: outfitting explorers foolish—or brave—enough to venture into the Trackless Sea, the Moonshae Isles, Maztica, and most famously, Chult.

Unlike ordinary markets, merchants here are accustomed to adventurers. Rather than attempting to sell extravagant luxuries, they instead offer practical advice, cautionary tales, and equipment intended to keep travelers alive.

Should the players seek recommendations, any local points them toward **Expedition Row**, a broad avenue lined with outfitters, herbalists, mapmakers, chandlers, and armorers.

---

## Read Aloud

> Beyond the warehouses nearest the docks, the streets widen once more into a district unlike the bustling merchant quarter behind you.
> 
> Here, storefronts display waterproof cloaks instead of silks, climbing gear instead of jewelry, and bundles of tightly packed trail rations instead of fresh bread. Brass compasses spin lazily in shop windows while rolled maps fill racks outside weathered storefronts.
> 
> Painted signs advertise cures for jungle fever, antidotes to serpent venom, insect repellents, and waterproof journals.
> 
> Many of the merchants watch newcomers with expressions somewhere between sympathy and resignation.
> 
> Several of them seem to recognize the destination before a single word is spoken.

---

## Running the Market

Allow the players to browse freely.

Most shopkeepers eventually ask the same question.

> "Where are you headed?"

Once the answer is "Chult," nearly every merchant pauses before continuing.

This is not meant to frighten the players.

It is meant to establish a recurring theme.

**Everyone who has experience with Chult respects it.**

Inside, shelves are meticulously organized.

> The scent of leather and lamp oil greets you as you enter.

---

## DM Notes

Don't rush this section. Let the players browse, compare items, haggle if they wish, and ask questions about Chult. Every merchant should leave them with the same impression: no one tries to scare them, but everyone who has experience with Chult treats it with quiet respect.

By the time they leave Expedition Row, the players should feel that they are no longer preparing for another adventure on the Sword Coast—they are preparing to enter a land where experience, caution, and endurance matter as much as courage.
