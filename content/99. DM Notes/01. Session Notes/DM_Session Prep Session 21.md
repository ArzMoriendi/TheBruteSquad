# Previous Session's Recap

After recovering the **Stoneheart Reliquary** and stepping through Mirabar's Harper portal, the Brute Squad expected to arrive near Neverwinter and continue their preparations for the Chult expedition. Instead, they emerged in **Primcross**, Leyawiin's hometown. What should have been a familiar homecoming quickly turned troubling as they discovered the hamlet shrouded in unease and learned that **Leyawiin's Literary Labyrinth** had been violently ransacked. Inside the ruined bookstore, the party found evidence of a targeted magical confrontation rather than a robbery. Most importantly, they discovered that **Sylvie was missing, but likely still alive**. A cryptic note left behind referenced _“The Lattice,”_ _“The Codex,”_ and Leyawiin's late mentor **Agatha**, while an unfamiliar sigil suggested the attackers had been searching for something specific hidden within the shop. The group soon learned from local elders that the symbol belonged to the **Githyanki**, dangerous planar raiders capable of traveling between worlds, raising the frightening possibility that Sylvie may have been taken beyond the Material Plane. Meanwhile, Veldar found trouble of his own after wandering to a tavern, drinking heavily, and successfully fighting off a would-be mugger before staggering back to the bookstore victorious, if somewhat battered.

The Brute Squad spent the remainder of the day investigating. They spoke with **Aldrich Sternfollow**, who confirmed the attack had occurred roughly two weeks earlier and that no useful trail had ever been found. Back at the bookstore, Bronn's searches revealed no hidden magical clues, while Sarhice dedicated himself to uncovering a rumored basement. The party examined Agatha's hidden tome and determined it radiated multiple schools of powerful magic, strengthening their suspicions that it was somehow connected to the mysterious Codex mentioned in the note. Leyawiin finally revealed that her original reason for leaving home had been to research this very artifact and prevent it from falling into the wrong hands. As night fell, the Brute Squad settled uneasily into the damaged bookstore, discussing whether to seek answers in Neverwinter or through their kobold allies in the Neverwinter Woods. With Sylvie missing, the Githyanki involved, and the mysterious Codex becoming increasingly important, the party ended the session facing one of the most personal and significant mysteries they had encountered so far.

After uncovering the truth behind the attack on Leyawiin's bookstore in Primcross, the party found themselves with more questions than answers. Sylvie was still missing, clues pointed toward a mysterious Codex and the Githyanki, and there was no reliable trail to follow. Before they departed, Aldrich Sternfollow, elder of Primcross, encouraged them to continue their journey rather than chase shadows. He promised to watch over Leyawiin's shop and assured her that if Sylvie returned, word would find her. With little left to discover in town, the Brute Squad turned their attention toward Neverwinter.

Arriving in Neverwinter was an experience all its own. The party passed through the crowded city gates, narrowly avoiding trouble with suspicious guards before invoking their connection to Boniface Abernathy. Inside, they were overwhelmed by the size of the city, its countless people, busy streets, and towering architecture. While exploring the Blue Lake district, Bronn experienced an unexpected encounter when a man named Edran recognized him as the son of his parents. Edran gifted him a black obsidian ring marked with the symbol of Shar and a cryptic note reading, “In the absence of light is truth.” The encounter left Bronn with far more questions than answers about his family and past. 

Eventually, the party reached the headquarters of the Luminous Meridian Trading Company and met with Boniface Abernathy. Boniface offered them a contract to travel to Chult, explore the uncharted wilderness, recover artifacts and knowledge, and report their discoveries back to the company. Despite the contract clearly favoring Boniface's interests, the Brute Squad negotiated what improvements they could and accepted. Each member received a signing bonus, the party received a provisioning fund, and passage was secured aboard the _Dawnmere_, captained by Seryn Vael'Thalor. Boniface warned them of increased undead activity in Chult and stressed the importance of hiring local guides once they arrived. 

Following the meeting, the party split up briefly. Bronn visited Neverdeath Graveyard, where he reaffirmed his faith in Kelemvor and received renewed divine favor. Veldar sought magical enhancements for Thumpy but discovered enchanting services in Neverwinter would be expensive and time consuming. The group ultimately decided Chult might provide better opportunities and began preparing for departure.

The following day, the Brute Squad headed to Neverwinter's harbor to locate the _Dawnmere_. There they learned from the harbor master that the ship would not board passengers until the next morning and that they should spend their remaining time preparing for the journey ahead. Zwargie, however, was unwilling to wait. Determined to find the ship himself, he spent nearly an hour searching the harbor before learning the _Dawnmere_ was anchored offshore. Deciding this was merely an obstacle rather than a warning sign, Zwarg attempted to swim out to it. Unfortunately, endurance was not on his side, and he ended up being rescued and returned to shore by local fishermen before ever reaching the vessel. 

Meanwhile, the rest of the party shopped throughout Neverwinter's expedition district. They purchased hammocks, mosquito netting, waterproof cloaks, climbing gear, rope, and sailor survival kits. Merchants repeatedly warned them that Chult was harsh, wet, dangerous, and largely unmapped. Reliable maps proved impossible to obtain, reinforcing the party's decision to hire guides once they reached Port Nyanzaru. Veldar also purchased a compass which he became convinced would somehow help him find his lost tribe. Bronn acquired a cask of quality wine and arranged for it to be delivered directly to the _Dawnmere_.

That evening, the party settled into the Mariner's Rest for one final comfortable night before departure. Over dinner and drinks they reviewed the expedition ahead, discussed supplies, debated Boniface's motivations, and prepared themselves mentally for the dangers awaiting them in Chult.

As everyone slept, GhoulMan underwent a powerful supernatural trial. Drawn into an impossible arena watched over by a celestial champion and an infernal warlord, he was tested repeatedly to determine whether he possessed the strength to endure suffering, defeat, and loss. His first challenge pitted him against **two Gnoll Pack Lords**, echoes of the creatures responsible for the destruction of his family and homeland. Though repeatedly beaten down, he refused to surrender. For his perseverance, he was granted the **Greatsword of Sharpness**. 

The second trial brought forth **two Gladiators**, veteran warriors who tested his endurance and martial skill. Again GhoulMan refused to yield, and the infernal judge rewarded him with **two Rings of Demonic Protection**, strengthening his defenses and hardening his resolve. 

Finally, the last gate opened and a towering **Minotaur Champion** entered the arena. Armed with his newfound gifts and fueled by sheer determination, GhoulMan fought the creature in a brutal duel and ultimately emerged victorious. As the Minotaur fell, the celestial and infernal judges declared that true strength is not measured by victory alone, but by the willingness to continue standing after every defeat. Satisfied with his performance, they released him from the vision, and GhoulMan awoke changed by the experience.

And so, as dawn broke over Neverwinter, the Brute Squad stood ready. Their supplies were packed. Their preparations complete. Ahead lay the _Dawnmere_, the Trackless Sea, and beyond it the deadly jungles of Chult, where ancient ruins, undead horrors, forgotten secrets, and perhaps answers to their personal mysteries waited to be found. The voyage begins now.
