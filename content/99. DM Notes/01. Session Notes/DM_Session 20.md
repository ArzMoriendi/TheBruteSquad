# Previous Session Recap

Previously on The Brute Squad...

Our courageous heroes have thus far rid the town of [[Erentir]] of a troublesome gang and its ruthless leader, explored an abandoned wizard's tower, befriended a troop of kobolds, battled a mage beneath a mountain, and earned the gratitude of the city of [[Mirabar]] for finding the cause of and ending attacks on supply caravans and their mining camp at [[Hammerfall Run]].

Most recently, they traveled to [[Primcross]], home of [[Leyawiin]], where they discovered her beloved bookstore in ruins and her sister, Sylvie, mysteriously missing. Clues left behind revealed the attack had been deliberate rather than a robbery, with arcane signs of battle and a cryptic note referencing "The Lattice," a mysterious Codex once entrusted to Leyawiin's mentor, [[Primcross|Agatha]]. The party learned that the strange sigil on the note belonged to the Githyanki, fearsome planar raiders, raising the chilling possibility that Sylvie had been taken beyond the Material Plane. With no trail to follow and little left to uncover, the Brute Squad resolved to press onward, hoping answers awaited them elsewhere.

Their search led them into the sprawling city of [[Neverwinter]], where new mysteries quickly found them. Bronn was approached by an old acquaintance of his parents, who entrusted him with an obsidian ring bearing the symbol of Shar and a cryptic message hinting that his family's past held secrets yet to be uncovered. Meanwhile, the party met once more with the flamboyant merchant [[Boniface Abernathy]], signing a lucrative—if heavily one-sided—contract to sail to [[Chult]] aboard the _Dawnmere_. Tasked with exploring the untamed jungle, recovering lost knowledge, and braving the growing undead threat, the Brute Squad now stands on the threshold of a dangerous new expedition. As they make their final preparations, unanswered questions surrounding Sylvie's fate, the enigmatic Codex, and Bronn's mysterious heritage promise that the greatest challenges may still lie ahead.

#  Scene Setter

**CHES 25**

The Brute Squad had arrived in [[Neverwinter]], reunited after the troubling discoveries in [[Primcross]]. [[Leyawiin]]'s bookstore had been ransacked, her sister Sylvie abducted, and clues pointed toward a mysterious Codex, planar forces, and the involvement of the Githyanki. With no immediate leads to pursue and an expedition contract already signed with [[Boniface Abernathy]], the party prepared to leave the [[Faerûn|Sword Coast]] behind and sail for the distant jungles of [[Chult]] aboard the _Dawnmere_. 

The Brute Squad made their way to Neverwinter's harbor to locate the ship that would carry them south. Upon arriving, they found the docks crowded with merchants, sailors, explorers, and vessels from across the Realms. After some questioning, the party located the harbor master, a busy half-orc managing the endless flow of cargo and passengers. He informed them that they had arrived early and that Captain [[Neverwinter|Seryn Vael'Thalor]] would not begin boarding until the following morning. He strongly advised them to spend their remaining coin preparing for Chult before departure. 

Not content to wait, [[Zwargie]] decided to locate the _Dawnmere_ himself. After spending nearly an hour searching the harbor, he eventually learned the vessel was anchored offshore rather than tied to a dock. Determined to reach it anyway, Zwarg attempted to swim out to the ship. His enthusiasm far exceeded his endurance. Before reaching the vessel, he exhausted himself and had to be rescued by passing fishermen, who hauled him back to shore and returned him to the docks considerably wetter than when he started. 

Meanwhile, the remainder of the Brute Squad entered Neverwinter's outfitter district. There they found merchants specializing in dangerous expeditions, offering supplies specifically intended for explorers traveling to hostile regions. After discussing the dangers of Chult and the challenges of jungle survival, the party purchased a variety of equipment including hammocks, mosquito netting, waterproof cloaks, climbing equipment, rope, and sailor survival kits required for ocean travel. [[Veldar]] also purchased a compass after becoming convinced it would somehow help him find his tribe and his chief. Whether the compass possessed any special qualities beyond pointing north remained uncertain.

While shopping, the party discussed survival strategies for Chult. They debated food, water, guides, jungle diseases, climbing gear, and methods of avoiding becoming meals for whatever predators awaited them in the jungle. Several merchants stressed the importance of hiring local guides once they arrived in [[Port Nyanzaru]], warning that maps of Chult were often outdated or incomplete and that much of the interior remained poorly documented.

[[Bronn the Humble|Bronn]] eventually wandered away from the outfitters and located a shipping supplier known as _The Whole Butt_. There he purchased a cask of quality wine and arranged for it to be delivered directly to the _Dawnmere_. When the merchant questioned whether Captain Vael'Thalor had approved the shipment, Bronn confidently assured him that all necessary permissions had already been granted. Somehow, the arrangement proceeded without further questions. 

The party then visited a cartographer seeking reliable maps of Chult. The proprietor regrettably informed them that no truly current maps existed. He offered journals and travel notes from explorers who had returned from the jungle, but the cost was considerable. Ultimately, the Brute Squad declined the purchase, deciding to rely upon local expertise once they reached Chult. 

With preparations completed, the party secured rooms at the *Mariner's Rest*, a comfortable inn near the harbor. Over food, drinks, baths, and conversation, they reviewed their plans for Chult and discussed the expedition contract they had signed with Boniface Abernathy. While opinions varied regarding whether the merchant truly had their best interests at heart, the party ultimately agreed to continue forward and honor their commitment.

That night, while the city slept, [[GhoulMan]] experienced a vision unlike any he had faced before.

The world around him fell away and he found himself within an impossible arena stretching beyond mortal sight. Watching over the arena were two powerful entities: one a shining armored champion representing perseverance, honor, and discipline; the other an infernal warlord embodying rage, survival, strength, and determination. Together they declared that GhoulMan would face a series of trials to determine whether he possessed the will to rise again after suffering, defeat, and death.

The first gates opened and two Gnoll Pack Lords emerged. These creatures immediately reminded GhoulMan of the gnolls responsible for the destruction of his homeland and the deaths of his family. The battle was brutal. Though he repeatedly fell, he refused every opportunity to surrender and continued fighting whenever offered the choice. Impressed by his refusal to quit, the armored champion rewarded him with the legendary Greatsword of Sharpness and instructed him to continue the trial. 

The second challenge brought forth two Gladiators armed with spear and shield. These veteran warriors tested GhoulMan's endurance, toughness, and ability to continue fighting despite overwhelming punishment. Once again he was beaten down, and once again he chose to continue. Recognizing his determination, the infernal warlord gifted him two Rings of Demonic Protection, dramatically increasing his resilience and defensive abilities. 

The final gates rose and revealed a massive Minotaur Champion, larger and more powerful than any opponent before it. Empowered by the gifts earned through the previous trials, GhoulMan entered a savage duel against the towering beast. The two exchanged devastating blows as the arena watched in silence. Ultimately, GhoulMan prevailed. Wielding the Greatsword of Sharpness, he delivered the final killing strike and brought the Minotaur low.

As the Minotaur vanished, the arena grew quiet. The two extraplanar judges approached and declared that true strength is not measured merely by victory, but by the willingness to continue standing after loss, failure, suffering, and death. GhoulMan had repeatedly chosen to continue where others would have yielded. Satisfied that he had proven worthy, the celestial and infernal powers allowed the vision to end. GhoulMan awoke in his room at the Mariner's Rest profoundly changed by the experience.
