# Previous Sessions Recap

Our last two sessions found our heroes deep beneath the ruined mining outpost of Hammerfall Run, standing within a forgotten ritual chamber carved from ancient stone. There, amidst shattered goliath zombies and towering natural pillars, the Brute Squad confronted a Thayan mage attempting to corrupt a mysterious relic of stone and ruby pulsing with unnatural power. The battle nearly broke them. GhoulMan, newly arrived through a planar rift and still reeling from the destruction of his homeland, was magically turned against the party. Veldar was frozen solid beneath a devastating Cone of Cold, and the mage herself revealed troubling knowledge of Zwargie and relics stolen long before. Yet through stubborn resolve, steel, spellcraft, and sheer will, the Brute Squad survived. The mage escaped into the darkness, but the chamber — and the strange artifact within it — belonged to the party.

That night, resting within the chamber itself, each member of the Brute Squad experienced visions, revelations, or transformations. Veldar again felt the primal presence of the Beast strengthening within him. Zwargie heard the approving whispers of the mysterious power guiding him. GhoulMan relived the slaughter of his wife and children at the hands of invading gnolls, awakening more determined and dangerous than before. Bronn rose spiritually enlightened beneath the quiet guidance of Kelemvor, while Leyawiin alone awoke uncertain, realizing the path of the Bladesinger no longer felt like her own. When at last they left Hammerfall Run behind, the mountain itself seemed intent on claiming them. A brutal blizzard trapped them upon the slopes, and even after the storm broke, avalanche and falling stone battered the party during their descent toward Mirabar.

Upon returning to the great mountain city, the Brute Squad was welcomed not as suspects, but as honored adventurers. Before Justiciar Geldryn Axebinder, Durni Copperpick confirmed their deeds within Hammerfall Run, and Veldar was finally cleared of all suspicion. When the party presented the relic they had recovered, Geldryn revealed its true identity: the legendary Stoneheart Reliquary, a mythical dwarven artifact capable of transmuting common ore into its purest form. Understanding the danger and significance of the relic, the party entrusted it to Mirabar’s protection and were rewarded richly for their service — gold, a Bag of Holding, noble travel papers bearing the Justiciar’s seal, access to Mirabar’s libraries, and future use of the city’s portal chambers. In the libraries, Leyawiin found clarity under the stern instruction of the dwarven evoker Harbek Bronzewarden, choosing at last to abandon Bladesinging and embrace the path of Evocation magic.

With their newfound status came smaller, stranger adventures throughout Mirabar itself. The party debated how best to profit from the decaying heads they had been carrying since their earlier exploits, eventually selling the head of Kragnoroth the Drunk through Mirabar’s shadowy black market contacts for a reduced bounty. Bronn and GhoulMan ventured into the dwarven undercity in search of dire rams and instead found Ironwool Stables, where Bronn attempted — unsuccessfully, though not without compassion — to heal the gravely ill mother of the stablekeeper Dagna. Moved by his efforts, she sold him an aging but spirited ram named Geezer and promised him a favor yet to be claimed. After final purchases, reforged equipment, and one last meeting with the Justiciar, the Brute Squad chose their next destination: Neverwinter. Armed with Geldryn’s letter of certification, the party traveled through a Harper portal alongside Geezer himself, emerging safely at a hidden Harper station just outside the great coastal city. And there, standing upon unfamiliar soil with roads, seas, and fresh dangers before them, our heroes prepared to take their next steps into a wider world.
