# Previous Session's Recap

After recovering the Stoneheart Reliquary and traveling through the Harper portal network, the Brute Squad unexpectedly arrived in **Primcross**, Leyawiin's hometown. There they discovered that Leyawiin's bookstore had been ransacked and her sister **Sylvie** had vanished. Evidence pointed to a targeted magical attack rather than a robbery, and a cryptic note mentioning **The Lattice**, **The Codex**, and **Agatha** suggested the attackers were searching for a powerful artifact. Local elders identified the note's sigil as belonging to the **Githyanki**, raising fears that Sylvie may have been taken beyond the Material Plane. While the party searched for answers, Veldar survived a drunken confrontation with a would-be mugger, and Leyawiin revealed that her own quest began with a desire to understand the mysterious tome entrusted to her by Agatha.

With no clear trail to follow, the party departed for **Neverwinter**, where they encountered both opportunity and new mysteries. Bronn was approached by a man named Edran, who recognized his family and gifted him an obsidian ring bearing the symbol of **Shar** along with a cryptic message about truth in darkness. Shortly thereafter, the party met **Boniface Abernathy** of the Luminous Meridian Trading Company and accepted a lucrative but one-sided contract to travel to **Chult**, explore its untamed wilderness, and recover artifacts and knowledge. Despite lingering concerns surrounding Sylvie and the Codex, the Brute Squad agreed that the expedition represented their best path forward.

Over the next day, the party prepared for the voyage south. They purchased survival gear, learned that reliable maps of Chult were nearly impossible to acquire, and secured passage aboard the _Dawnmere_. Zwarg unsuccessfully attempted to swim to the ship before being rescued by fishermen, while GhoulMan experienced a powerful supernatural vision in which celestial and infernal entities tested his resolve through a series of brutal combats. By overcoming gnoll pack lords, gladiators, and a mighty minotaur champion, he earned a **Greatsword of Sharpness**, **two Rings of Demonic Protection**, and the respect of his mysterious judges. As dawn broke, the Brute Squad stood ready to leave the Sword Coast behind and begin their journey toward Chult, carrying with them unanswered questions about Sylvie, the Codex, Bronn's past, and the dangers that awaited in the jungle.
