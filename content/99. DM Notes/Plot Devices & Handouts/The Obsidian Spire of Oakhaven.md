**The Obsidian Spire of Oakhaven**

_(Rebalanced specifically for a 2-Player Party: Level 4 Wizard & Paladin)_

  

## Dungeon Overview

- **Party Size:** 2 Players (Level 4 Wizard & Paladin)
    
      
    
- **Theme:** Petrified flora, warped arcana, fey containment
    
      
    
- **Primary Objective:** Rescue an ally locked within the roots beneath the Obsidian Spire.
    
      
    
- **Environmental Rules:**
    
      
    - _Fey Surge:_ Whenever a spell of 1st level or higher is cast, roll a d20. On a **1–2**, a harmless cosmetic surge occurs (e.g., flowers bloom instantly on nearby roots, ambient light shifts colors, or shadows whisper).
        
          
        
    - _Short Rest Safety:_ Room 2 can be locked from the inside once cleared, allowing the party to take a single Short Rest safely.
        
          
        

## Dungeon Layout & Rooms

### Room 1: The Root Gate

A cavernous hall choked with glass-like petrified roots.

  

- **Encounter:** **1 Feywild Ghoul** prowls the upper ledges.
    
      
    
- **Hazard:** Crossing the room requires spotting **Thorn-Traps** (DC 12 Wisdom/Perception to spot, DC 12 Dexterity save or take 1d6 piercing damage and become restrained until an action is spent breaking free).
    
      
    

### Room 2: The Siphon Chamber

A circular stone room with a glowing purple crystal floating over a dark pool.

  

- **Puzzle Mechanism:** To disable the barrier leading to Room 4, the party must align three rotating stone dials set into the roots. A DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation) check reveals the sequence.
    
      
    
- **Failure Penalty:** A failed check causes the crystal to pulse, dealing 1d6 force damage to all creatures within 15 feet.
    
      
    

### Room 3: The Warden’s Menagerie

Petrified wood cages housing feral Feywild beasts used to hunt down runaways.

  

- **Encounter:** **2 Steam Mephits** guarding the walkways. They use hit-and-run tactics to spray steam at the Paladin while attempting to close distance with the Wizard.
    
      
    

### Room 4: The Core Vault

A massive root cavern beneath the Spire. A cage woven from ironwood hangs over a chasm, containing the captive ally.

  

- **Encounter:** **Thalior the Shadow-Weaver** guards the cage alone.

- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
    
      
    
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
    
      
    
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
    
      
    
- **Languages:** Common
    
      
    

**Actions**

  

- **Claws:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 7 (2d4 + 2) slashing damage. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
    
      
    
- **Fey Step (1/Day):** The ghoul teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Saving Throws:** Int +5, Wis +3

- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13

**Traits**

  

- **Fey Step (Recharge 5–6):** As a bonus action, Thalior teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see.
    
      
    

**Spellcasting**

Thalior is a 4th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit with spell attacks).

  

- **Cantrips (at will):** _Ray of Frost_, _Minor Illusion_
    
      
    
- **1st level (4 slots):** _Shield_, _Magic Missile_, _Thunderwave_
    
      
    
- **2nd level (2 slots):** _Misty Step_, _Hold Person_
    
      
    

**Actions**

  

- **Shadow-Woven Staff:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) psychic damage.
    
      
    

**Lair Action**

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Thalior can trigger one of the following effects:

  

- **Root Grasp:** Petrified roots sprout under one player. The target must succeed on a DC 11 Strength saving throw or be restrained until initiative 20 on the next round. (A player can use an action to break free).
