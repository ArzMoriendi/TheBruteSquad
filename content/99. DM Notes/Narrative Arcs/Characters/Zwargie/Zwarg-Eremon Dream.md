



# Dream Sequence

## Walk With Me

### DM Notes

This dream occurs during Zwarg's first night aboard the _Dawnmere_.

The tone should be peaceful from beginning to end. Speak slowly. Leave pauses between descriptions. There is no urgency here.

Eremon is not trying to recruit Zwarg.

He is simply allowing Zwarg to witness the kind of guardian he believes the world needs.

---

## Read Aloud

> Sleep comes easily beneath the gentle rocking of the _Dawnmere_.> 
> The steady rhythm of waves against the hull slowly fades.> 
> The creaking of timbers disappears.> 
> Even the scent of salt gives way to something warmer.
> 
> Like morning. Like fresh rain. Like....  Wildflowers.
> 
> The comforting warmth of the first sunrise after a long night.
> 
> You find yourself standing atop a gently sloping hillside overlooking a broad valley bathed in the golden light of dawn.
> A crystal stream winds lazily through fields of wildflowers, catching the sunlight like ribbons of gold. Ancient oak trees stand watch over neat farms and quiet cottages, their leaves dancing in a gentle breeze.
> In the distance, you see half-orc farmers laugh together as they begin their morning's work. Children race through the tall grass chasing one another while elders sit upon worn wooden porches, watching another peaceful day begin.
> 
> The world feels...
> 
> Whole.
> 
> Safe.
> 
> As though nothing wicked has ever touched this place.

> 
> A crystal stream winds lazily through fields of wildflowers, catching the sunlight like ribbons of gold. Ancient oak trees stand watch over neat farms and quiet cottages, their leaves dancing in a gentle breeze.

> 
> Whole.
> 
> Safe.
>

Allow Zwarg a moment to take in the scene.

Then continue.

---

## Read Aloud

> Standing a short distance away is a lone man.
> 
> He appears entirely human.
> 
> He wears polished plate armor that catches the morning sun until it seems less forged than illuminated. Every movement is calm and deliberate, carrying the quiet confidence of someone who has never needed to prove his strength.
> 
> Resting beside him is a beautifully crafted glaive, its broad blade planted lightly into the earth as though it weighs nothing at all.
> 
> Though he has not yet turned toward you...
> 
> Something about him feels strangely familiar.
> 
> Then he speaks.
> 
> The voice is unmistakable.
> 
> It is the same voice that has whispered within your thoughts before.
> 
> Only now...
> 
> It is no whisper.
> 
> It is clear.
> 
> Strong.
> 
> Confident.
> 
> Warm.
> 
> Every word carries quiet conviction.
> 
> "Walk with me."

The two of you begin walking.

For several moments...

Neither of you speaks.

Only birdsong accompanies your footsteps.

Eventually, he breaks the silence.

> "Beautiful, isn't it?"
> 
> _(He gestures across the valley.)_
> 
> "Do you know why it remains so?"

Allow Zwarg to answer however he wishes.

Regardless of his response, the man nods approvingly.

> "Because someone was willing to stand between this..."
> 
> _(He gestures toward the valley.)_
> 
> "...and those who would destroy it."

The two of you continue down the hillside.

---

## Read Aloud

> Ahead, a frightened half-orc family rushes along the road.
> 
> A mother clutches a small child tightly against her chest while an elderly man struggles to keep pace beside them.
> 
> Behind them come three brigands.
> 
> A broad-shouldered human strides confidently at their head, a cruel smile spread across his face.
> 
> At his side runs a lean half-elf, twirling a dagger between practiced fingers.
> 
> Bringing up the rear is a scarred half-orc carrying a heavy axe across one shoulder.
> 
> The half-elf drags a terrified young half-orc girl by the wrist.
> 
> The human laughs as the elderly man stumbles to his knees.
> 
> The family does not see you.
> 
> They do not see the armored man beside you.
> 
> They simply run.

Without hesitation...

The stranger steps into the middle of the road.

The brigands stop.

The human sneers.

The half-elf chuckles under his breath.

The half-orc shifts his grip upon his axe but says nothing.

The human slowly raises a loaded crossbow.

> "Move."

The stranger does not answer.

He simply exhales.

---

## Read Aloud

> The morning grows brighter.
> 
> At first, you think the sun has emerged from behind a passing cloud.
> 
> Then you realize...
> 
> The light is coming from him.
> 
> A brilliant radiance pours from every seam of his armor, bathing the valley in warm golden light. Every shadow retreats before it, and the air itself seems to hum with quiet power.
> 
> Wildflowers bloom where he stands.
> 
> The grass bends gently away from him as though touched by an unseen breeze.
> 
> The brilliance becomes so intense that the brigands instinctively shield their eyes.
> 
> The half-elf drops his dagger.
> 
> The half-orc stumbles backward, shielding his face.
> 
> The human looses his crossbow bolt.

> The bolt streaks through the air.
> 
> Before it reaches him...
> 
> It dissolves into countless motes of golden light.

The stranger calmly reaches down.

He lifts the glaive effortlessly into one hand.

Its blade shines with the same warm brilliance as the rising sun.

There is no flourish.

No battle cry.

Only absolute certainty.

---

The human's courage finally breaks.

He falls to his knees.

His crossbow clatters harmlessly onto the road.

> "Please..."
> 
> "Mercy..."

The stranger studies him quietly.

Not with anger.

Not with hatred.

Only complete understanding.

Finally, he speaks.

> "When she begged..."
> 
> "...did you listen?"

The human lowers his head.

Silence answers the question.

The stranger slowly turns toward you.

For the first time, you clearly see his face.

Your eyes are immediately drawn to his own.

They are unlike anything you have ever seen.

Within them lies the horizon at dawn—the final stars of night suspended against a sky already surrendering to the rising sun. Tiny points of silver shimmer within endless amber, as though the promise of every sunrise had been captured within living eyes.

Looking into them does not feel like being judged.

It feels...

As though every failure...

Every triumph...

Every hope...

Every regret...

Has been seen completely.

And accepted.

He speaks.

> "Mercy is sacred."
> 
> "It cannot exist without justice."
> 
> "To spare the unrepentant at the cost of the innocent..."
> 
> "...is not compassion."
> 
> "...it is surrender."

With one smooth motion...

The glaive sweeps through the morning air.

A single perfect strike.

The sunlight seems to linger behind its arc.

The human falls.

The half-elf and the half-orc stare in stunned disbelief for only a heartbeat before turning and fleeing down the road, abandoning both their companion and their victims.

The stranger makes no effort to pursue them.

Instead...

He walks quietly to the frightened half-orc child.

He kneels.

Helping her gently to her feet before returning her safely to her mother's embrace.

The family bows deeply in gratitude before continuing down the road.

Only after they are safely gone does he clean the blade and rest the glaive once more upon his shoulder.

The valley is peaceful again.

Birdsong returns.

The breeze carries the scent of wildflowers once more.

The stranger turns back toward you.

The rising sun catches his eyes again.

For a heartbeat...

It is as though you are looking into the promise of every dawn yet to come.

He smiles.

Not with pride.

But with hope.

> "Every dawn is another chance to choose the light."

He extends one hand toward you.

> "Walk with me again."
