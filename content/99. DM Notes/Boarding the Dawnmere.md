Excellent. This is where the session transitions from introspection to commitment. The party has rested, GhoulMan has awoken from a dream that feels more real than waking life, and now they take their first steps aboard the _Dawnmere_. This scene should establish Captain Seryn Vael'Thalor as one of the most competent NPCs the party has met—someone they immediately trust, even if they cannot fully understand.

One recommendation before we begin: **do not play Seryn as mysterious for the sake of being mysterious.** Instead, play them as a consummate professional. The mystery comes naturally because Seryn never volunteers personal information, not because they evade questions. This makes the eventual loss of the _Dawnmere_ much more impactful, as the players will likely grow to respect both the captain and the crew.

# Scene Five

# Boarding the _Dawnmere_

## DM Notes

The morning dawns clear and cool over Neverwinter Harbor. The bustle of the previous day has given way to organized purpose. Sailors move with practiced efficiency, longshoremen finish loading cargo, and the smell of tar, fresh-cut timber, and salt fills the air.

This scene establishes three important things:

- The _Dawnmere_ is a well-run, professional vessel.
    
- Captain Seryn Vael'Thalor is calm, capable, and deeply respected.
    
- The voyage is not presented as dangerous. In fact, everything suggests it will be routine.
    

The contrast with the coming pirate attack—or whatever calamity ultimately befalls the ship—will make the eventual loss feel earned rather than arbitrary.

---

## Read Aloud

> Dawn breaks in shades of pale gold over Neverwinter Harbor as the city slowly awakens around you. Fishing vessels already drift toward open water while merchant crews shout final orders across crowded piers.
> 
> Ahead, tied securely alongside Pier Seven, rests the vessel that will carry you across the Trackless Sea.
> 
> The _Dawnmere_ is a handsome two-masted caravel, its hull painted a deep navy blue trimmed with polished oak. Brass fittings gleam in the morning sun, and every rope appears neatly coiled, every sail perfectly furled. The ship bears the emblem of the Luminous Meridian Trading Company upon its stern—a rising sun cresting over calm waters.
> 
> Sailors move with quiet purpose across her decks. No one shouts unnecessarily. Every order is answered with crisp efficiency.
> 
> It is immediately apparent that this is not merely a merchant vessel.
> 
> It is a ship whose crew takes pride in their work.

## The Crew

As the party approaches the gangplank, several sailors pause to acknowledge them with polite nods before returning immediately to their duties.

No one gawks at the adventurers.

No one seems impressed by weapons or armor.

To this crew, they are simply passengers.

---

## Bosun Garrik Stoneshield

A broad-shouldered mountain dwarf supervises the loading of cargo.

Without looking up, he calls to the party.

> "Mind the gangplank. Fresh tar this morning."

After a pause, he adds,

> "Captain will meet you on the quarterdeck."

# Captain Seryn Vael'Thalor

At the stern of the ship stands an elf whose presence quietly commands attention.

---

## Read Aloud

> Standing upon the quarterdeck is a tall elf dressed in a finely tailored navy coat embroidered with silver thread depicting waves and stars. Dark hair, touched faintly with silver despite an otherwise youthful appearance, is neatly tied behind their head.
> 
> Their posture is flawless.
> 
> Hands rest calmly behind their back as they study the harbor with quiet focus.
> 
> They neither pace nor fidget.
> 
> They simply observe.
> 
> At first glance, nothing about them appears particularly extraordinary.
> 
> Yet as your eyes linger, you realize something difficult to explain.
> 
> Their features possess an almost impossible balance of masculine and feminine qualities.
> 
> The voice, when it finally comes, is smooth and measured—neither distinctly one nor the other.
> 
> Every movement seems deliberate.
> 
> Every word carefully chosen.

---

## First Greeting

Seryn turns as the party approaches and inclines their head in a respectful greeting.

> "Welcome aboard the _Dawnmere_."
> 
> "I am Captain Seryn Vael'Thalor."
> 
> "By contract with the Luminous Meridian Trading Company, I have the honor—and the responsibility—of conveying you safely to Port Nyanzaru."
> 
> _(A brief pause.)_
> 
> "While you remain aboard this vessel, there are but three rules."
> 
> Seryn raises one finger.
> 
> "Respect the sea."
> 
> A second.
> 
> "Respect the crew."
> 
> A third.
> 
> "Do nothing that needlessly endangers either."
> 
> A faint smile crosses the captain's face.
> 
> "If you can abide by those principles, I suspect we shall enjoy one another's company."

---

## Captain's Briefing

Seryn gestures toward the main deck.

> "You are free to move about the vessel, though I ask that you avoid interfering with the crew while we are underway."
> 
> "Meals are served shortly after sunrise and again near sunset."
> 
> "Should rough weather arise, my officers will provide instruction."
> 
> "Should danger arise..."
> 
> The captain's expression never changes.
> 
> "...I will do the same."

---

# If the Party Probes the Captain

Seryn answers every question honestly, but never expansively.

## "How long have you been sailing?"

> "Long enough to know that the sea rewards humility."

---

## "Have you sailed to Chult before?"

> "Several times."

A pause.

> "No two voyages have ever been alike."

---

## "What dangers should we expect?"

> "Storms."
> 
> "Disease."
> 
> "Pirates."
> 
> "Poor decisions."
> 
> "Only one of those is entirely within our control."

---

## "Have you ever lost a ship?"

Seryn studies the horizon for several moments.

> "...No."

Nothing more.

---

## "Why are you so difficult to read?"

For the first time, genuine amusement touches the captain's expression.

> "Years at sea."
> 
> "The ocean rarely rewards those who reveal every thought passing through their mind."

---

## "Are you a man or a woman?"

The captain regards the speaker for several silent seconds before offering the faintest hint of a smile.

> "An understandable question."
> 
> "One that has remarkably little bearing upon whether I can bring you safely to Chult."

Without irritation, Seryn smoothly redirects the conversation.

# Skill Checks

### Insight (DC 18)

Seryn speaks with absolute sincerity.

Nothing said thus far has been misleading.

---

### Insight (DC 23)

Success reveals something unexpected.

It is not that Seryn conceals their emotions.

Rather, years of command have taught them complete emotional discipline.

Whatever personal history, fears, or ambitions the captain possesses remain hidden beneath a lifetime of self-control.

Determining Seryn's gender proves equally impossible—not because of deception, but because every aspect of their presentation has become secondary to the role of captain.

---

### Perception (DC 17)

The crew occasionally glance toward Seryn when making decisions, even without being instructed.

Their confidence in the captain borders on absolute.

## Departure

As the final crates are secured, Bosun Garrik calls across the deck.

> "All cargo accounted for, Captain!"

Seryn nods once.

The response is immediate.

> "Very good."
> 
> _(The captain turns toward the crew.)_
> 
> "Cast off."
