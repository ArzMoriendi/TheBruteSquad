# THE BRUTE SQUAD

## Chapter VIII – Across the Trackless Sea

### Session 20 – _The Last Peace Before Chult_

---

# Adventure Summary

With their business in Neverwinter concluded and their passage aboard the merchant vessel _Dawnmere_ secured, the Brute Squad prepares for the longest journey of their lives. Before they ever glimpse the jungles of Chult, however, the sea itself begins changing them.

While gathering supplies and making their final preparations, old powers begin taking notice. GhoulMan is pulled beyond the veil by two ancient forces whose rivalry has shaped countless wars. Zwarg walks beside a celestial whose ideals burn brighter than the sun itself. Bronn begins noticing unsettling signs below deck that suggest death may already have found passage aboard the _Dawnmere_.

Though no swords need be drawn during this voyage's opening days, every conversation and every dream quietly shifts the course of the campaign. By session's end, the peace of the Trackless Sea will be shattered, leaving the heroes staring into the beginning of a battle they never expected.

---

# Adventure Themes

This session revolves around three themes.

**Preparation.**  
Every NPC reminds the party that Chult rewards those who prepare and punishes those who do not.

**Choice.**  
Every major NPC presents a path rather than an order.

**Crossing Thresholds.**  
Neverwinter is the last truly civilized place the party may see for many months.

# Neverwinter Harbor

### Read Aloud

> The streets gradually widen as the smell of salt replaces the scent of stone and hearthfires. Buildings become shorter, warehouses replace homes, and the cries of merchants give way to shouted orders carried across the wind.
> 
> Soon the harbor comes into view.
> 
> Hundreds of vessels crowd Neverwinter's sprawling docks. Fishing boats unload their morning catches while broad merchant cogs creak beneath impossible stacks of cargo. Longshoremen haul barrels of wine, crates of spices, coils of rope, and cages filled with brightly colored birds whose strange calls echo over the water.
> 
> Somewhere nearby, a blacksmith hammers glowing iron into anchors. Gulls circle endlessly overhead, diving toward scraps thrown from fishmongers' stalls before angry curses send them skyward once more.
> 
> Beyond the nearest piers, larger ships ride peacefully at anchor upon the gray-blue waters of the Trackless Sea. Their masts sway gently together like a forest bending beneath a steady breeze.
> 
> It is a place filled with movement, noise, commerce...
> 
> ...and departures.

## General Atmosphere

Unlike Mirabar, Neverwinter's docks never truly rest.

People from dozens of cultures mingle together.

The party notices:

- Chultan traders wearing colorful woven fabrics.
    
- Shield Dwarves supervising heavy cargo.
    
- Moon Elves unloading delicate crates.
    
- Weathered sailors with tattoos from every corner of Faerûn.
    
- A cage holding several screeching monkeys destined for some wealthy noble.
    

Characters proficient in History or Nature (DC 13) recognize several goods that could only have come from Chult.

These include:

- bundles of teak
    
- giant orchid blossoms
    
- dinosaur hides
    
- fragrant spices
    
- strange fruit larger than melons

# Finding the Dockmaster

The _Dawnmere_ rests nearly four piers away.

Before anyone approaches her, a booming voice cuts across the dock.

---

## Dockmaster

**Urgash Stonewake**

Half-Orc

Dockmaster of Pier Seven

Former Bosun

### Appearance

Urgash stands nearly seven feet tall, broad even for a half-orc. His dark green skin has faded with age, weathered by decades beneath salt spray and harsh sunlight. His left hand is missing two fingers, while a white scar disappears beneath one eye and into his thick black beard.

His clothes are practical rather than impressive.

A heavy ledger hangs beneath one arm.

Everything about him says he values efficiency over conversation.

---

### Read Aloud

> A towering half-orc steps between you and the pier, scratching something into a weathered ledger with surprising care.
> 
> Without looking up he rumbles,
> 
> "Names."

After recording them, he finally looks up.

His expression never changes.

---

### Urgash's Dialogue

> "You're the adventurers Boniface mentioned."
> 
> _(He glances toward the harbor.)_
> 
> "Passage aboard the _Dawnmere_."
> 
> "You're early."
> 
> "Captain Vael'Thalor doesn't board passengers until morning."
> 
> _(He closes the ledger with a loud thump.)_
> 
> "Good."
> 
> "Means you've still got time to prepare."

If anyone asks why:

> "Because Chult isn't the Sword Coast."
> 
> "Steel's useful."
> 
> "Won't save you from fever."
> 
> "Won't keep the insects off."
> 
> "Won't help when the rain doesn't stop for ten days."

He gestures toward the market district.

> "Expedition outfitters are three streets east."
> 
> "Spend your coin now."
> 
> "Out there..."
> 
> _(He points toward the endless western sea.)_
> 
> "...coin doesn't buy much."

If asked about Captain Seryn:

Urgash pauses for several seconds.

> "Best captain I've worked with."
> 
> "Never lost a ship."
> 
> "Never abandoned a sailor."
> 
> "Never tells anyone more than they need to know."

If pressed further:

> "You'll meet the captain tomorrow."
> 
> "Until then..."
> 
> "Buy supplies."
> 
> "Find a bed."
> 
> "Enjoy your last quiet night."

Conversation over.

Urgash immediately returns to his ledger.
