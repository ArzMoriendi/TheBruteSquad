### Traits

**_Flyby._** The gargoyle doesn’t provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy’s reach.**_Basalt Resilience._** If damage is reduced by its damage resistance, it ignores the first 3 damage of that hit.

  

### Actions

**_Multiattack._** The gargoyle makes four attacks: two with its Claws, one with its Bite, and one with its Tail Slam. **_Claw._** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 9 (1d10 + 4) Slashing damage. **_Bite._** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 10 (1d12 + 4) Piercing damage. **_Tail Slam._** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away and have the **Prone** condition.

  

### Bonus Actions

**_Stone Camouflage._** The gargoyle drops prone and blends into the basalt wall. Until it moves or attacks, it has Advantage on initiative or attack rolls for its next strike.

  

### Reactions

**_Reactive Swipe._** When a creature misses a melee attack against the gargoyle while within 5 feet of it, the gargoyle makes a single Claw attack against that attacker.
