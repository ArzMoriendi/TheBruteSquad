## Backstory

Gogz is a tinkerer that deals in machines mostly now, thanks to a fireworks "incident." His right arm to the shoulder and right leg up to mid-thigh were blown off after some aggressive black powder experimentation and replaced with some mechanical prosthetics of his own design. He was originally from the Neverwinter Wood, but when he realized he had a knack for engineering he moved into Neverwinter proper to apprentice at a fireworks shop, while working on tinkering projects in his free time. Formerly of the Cragmaw Tribe growing up, goblins don't have specific parents and are raised communally.

- Current Goal (s): 
	- Make Money
	- Tinker with things
