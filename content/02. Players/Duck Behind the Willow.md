## Backstory

The Jinx of the Salty Yarn
Ducky wasn’t born a Monk; she was born a disaster.

As a kitten, she was the "Social Director" of a merchant vessel called the Salty Yarn. She was the heart of the ship—playful, clumsy, and convinced that every stranger was just a friend she hadn't tripped over yet. But for a sailor, "clumsiness" is just another word for "curse." Ducky’s life changed the night she saw a shiny brass lynchpin rolling across the deck during a midnight gale. She pounced, missed, and crashed into the emergency rigging. In a chaotic chain reaction of orange fur and failing wood, she accidentally triggered the lifeboat release. The ship’s emergency rations and half their survival gear vanished into the black Atlantic.

The crew didn't see a playful cat anymore; they saw a Jinx.

When the ship docked, the Captain’s cold glare told her everything. Terrified and heartbroken, Ducky bolted. As she leaped from the deck to the pier, she crashed through a massive rack of drying fishing nets, decorative silk signal flags, and loose yarn. She didn't break through—she became the center of the knot. Too scared to stop, she ran for days as a sentient tumbleweed of silk and hemp until she reached the mountains.

The Three-Day "Meditation"
Exhausted and trapped in her "web of mistakes," Ducky crawled under the porch of the Monastery of the Still Mind. She was so traumatized by her own clumsiness that she stayed perfectly still for 72 hours, terrified that moving a single claw would break the world.

The Monks found her and were stunned. They had never seen such "discipline." They believed this young Tabaxi had achieved a state of Perfect Stillness. They took her in, honoring her "sacred robes" (the tangled mess of nets). Ducky was too shy and ashamed to tell them she was actually just having a three-day panic attack.

The Fairy Collision
During her first attempt at "Walking Meditation," Ducky saw a long, green garden hose that looked suspiciously like a sea serpent. Her "Zoomies" triggered instantly. She became an orange blur, colliding head-on with Barnaby, a grumpy Fairy scholar out gathering magical components.

Barnaby was scooped up into the eye of her netting like a bug in a windshield. He tried to cast a spell to escape, but the "Jinx" was too strong—he accidentally bound his only spell, Haste, to her ribbons. Now, they are stuck together: a clumsy cat trying to be "still," and a tiny, angry engine of speed screaming for his life.

Ducky’s Character Snapshot
Category	Description
The "Voice"	Barnaby the Fairy. Ducky thinks he’s her "Ancestral Conscience." He screams "GO, YOU ORANGE FOOL!" to trigger her speed.
