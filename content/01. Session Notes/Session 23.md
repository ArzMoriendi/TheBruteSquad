# Previous Session Recap

The Brute Squad began last session at the Harper safe house portal in Primcross, using a magic mirror to consult regional coordinator Harper Brightcandle Resmythe Winphala. Learning that their requested expert on Githyanki was unavailable for six months, Winphala directed them to Neverwinter’s Blacklake District to find a guide. There, the party tracked down Gogz, an eccentric goblin tinkerer with mechanical prosthetics, and successfully recruited him for their adventure.  Prior to departing, he picked up some black powder for tinkering.  The part caught a griffin-pulled carriage transport arranged by the Harpers to the archaeological dig site near Mount Hotenow.

Upon arriving at the dig camp, the party descended into the ruin and encountered a series of ancient and hazardous obstacles. Using Gogz's battle-boar automaton, Kevin, they safely tested, and passed, a sequence of mechanical traps, pressure plates, pendulum blades, and arcane electrical traps. They then crossed a perilous, yawning chasm spanned by 12 staggered, spinning disc platforms, utilizing Ducky's and Sarhice's acrobatics and the barbarians' strength to navigate the gap safely.

Reaching the inner chambers, the Brute Squad encountered the rock gnome archaeologist Felcryn Flukereach and his remaining crew studying a glowing barrier before a black basalt wall. After deciphering a Sylvan riddle about empty vessels, Gogz sent Kevin through safely while an unfortunate assistant perished upon touching it. Following a second riddle requiring a strike of raw, dark will, Sarhice stepped through the barrier unharmed, drew his blade, infused it with magical power, and struck the black basalt wall.  This shattered the arcane seal and opening the path forward into the depths of the facility.

#  Scene Setter

We find our adventurous heroes inside the spire near Mt. Hotenow...

Picking up right where they left off, the party stood before the newly unsealed basalt passage alongside the rock gnome archaeologist, Felcryn Flukereach, and his surviving, shaken interns who were eager to avoid any more murderous traps.

Capitalizing on the moment, Felcryn struck a bargain with the group: if the Brute Squad would clear the way of dangers for him, he would provide any help or information he may have on the Githyanki to aid the party in tracking down Leyawiin's missing sister, Sylvie.

With a deal struck, the party descended into the dark, stale basalt corridors, Veldar leading the way, Leyawiin scouting with Mage hand, and the party on high alert. Walking into the darkness, they heard a crashing noise and realized they were sealed in.  As they advanced past smooth stone walls and metal floor gratings, Leyawiin encountered a weird barrier of types with her Mage hand.  Ducky loosed a stone from her slingshot, confirming that it wasn't a barrier, but a creature.  The party was facing a massive, stealthy Gelatinous Cube silently gliding in the absolute darkness.

Quick tactical coordination averted disaster as Ducky loosed sling shots, Gogz lobbed an improvised bean-can explosive that rippled through the acidic mass with a muffled underwater bubble effect, and Veldar and GhoulMan surged forward with crushing melee strikes. GhoulMan gave the finishing slice and the creature dissolved.  

Unwisely, Ducky tasted the remaining fluid of the cube, incurring some corrosive damage, but ultimately surviving. Gogz successfully salvaged five metal shards from ithe  remains.

Pushing deeper into the ancient temple, the party's investigations uncovered pristine, immaculately clean stonework and overhead floating lights that seemed to actively push back against an oppressive, enveloping darkness.  Continuing down the hallway, the party discovered a hidden wall panel equipped with Sylvan-inscribed levers, toggles, and switches that, when operated, restored hallway lighting and opened the closed doorway back to Felcryn's camp.

Reaching the end of the illuminated corridor, the Brute Squad found a pair of massive, intricately carved double doors featuring stone reliefs of two elven figure (one vibrant and showing life, one gaunt and fairly corpse-like) reaching hands to each other beneath a celestial light symbol.

Utilizing dexterous lockpicking and thieves' tools, Gogz unsealed the heavy doors, granting the party access to a wide, eight-sided octagonal basalt chamber centered around a massive structural pillar.

This inner area immediately tested the party with a deadly encounter: a ferocious ambush by a four-armed gargoyle guardian shifting down from the shadows above.

The brutal battle pushed the Brute Squad with multi-attacks, sweeping wing beats, and crushing tail slams that knocked fighters prone, leading to incredible tactical improvisation.  Leyawiin trying various spells with limited success, Veldar and Sarhice trying melee attacks, and Ducky grappling and hurling Kevin the battle-boar across the room like a ballistic projectile.

Amidst the chaos, Veldar unleashed a raging, feral visage with a spiny tail, Ghoulman delivered relentless slashing blows, and finally Leyawiin stepped forward to channel a resounding, bell-like magical knoll that shattered the stone gargoyle into dust midair, bringing a triumphant close to the session.
