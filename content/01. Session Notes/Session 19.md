# Previous Session Recap


 
Fresh from their triumph beneath [[Hammerfall Run]], the Brute Squad returned to [[Mirabar]] as honored heroes. The legendary  *Stoneheart Reliquary*was entrusted to the city's care, earning the party gold, prestige, and the gratitude of the dwarves, while each member found their own measure of growth. Leyawiin abandoned the path of the Bladesinger to embrace the destructive certainty of Evocation under [[Harbek Bronzewarden]]'s tutelage, Bronn received Kelemvor's blessing and newfound purpose, and the party spent their hard-earned downtime tending to unfinished business. Their search for a bounty on the infamous criminal [[Kragnoroth the Drunk]] led them from Mirabar's halls of justice into its hidden black market, where they managed to sell the pirate's decaying head for far less than hoped. Bronn's compassion also earned him an unexpected ally when his attempt to heal a dying dwarven woman failed to cure her mysterious illness, yet won him the loyal ram, Geezer, and a favor that may one day be repaid. With Mirabar's business concluded, the Brute Squad stepped through a Harper portal, expecting to arrive in or very near Neverwinter.

Instead, fate delivered them to Primcross, Leyawiin's quiet hometown, where an unsettling silence hung over familiar streets. Any thoughts or hopes of a joyful return home were shattered when they discovered Leyawiin's beloved bookstore ransacked and her sister, Sylvie, missing. Careful investigation revealed that this was not a simple burglary, but a calculated assault. A cryptic note spoke of awakening "the Lattice," finding a mysterious Codex once entrusted to Leyawiin's mentor, Agatha, and bore a sigil of Githyanki origin; planar raiders whose presence on the Material Plane raised troubling questions about where Sylvie had been taken. The hidden magical tome left behind, radiating layers of powerful enchantments, proved to be the likely object of the kidnappers' search, prompting Leyawiin to reveal that uncovering the truth behind this artifact had always been and underlying reason for her adventures.

As the party struggled to make sense of these revelations, danger lurked around nearby corners. Veldar, alone and lumbering drunkenly through the streets, survived a surprise ambush, felling his would-be mugger with "Thumpy" before staggering back to the ruined shop, hurt but triumphant. 

With no further clues to uncover, the companions settled in for an uneasy night among broken shelves and unanswered questions. Leyawiin mourning her missing sister, Bronn sleeping beside faithful Geezer, Zwarg finding comfort in the privy, GhoulMan wrestling with restless dreams, Veldar passed out from drink and battle, and Sarhice tirelessly digging for a basement that stubbornly refused to exist. Morning approaches, and with it a difficult choice: seek answers in the sprawling city of Neverwinter, or venture into the Neverwinter Wood in search of allies who may know more about the mysterious forces now moving against them.

#  Scene Setter

**CHES 26**

Morning came quietly to [[Primcross]], pale sunlight filtering through broken shutters and dust-choked air inside Leyawiin’s ruined shop. Outside, life pressed forward despite recent events; villagers resumed their routines, carts creaked along worn paths, and distant bells from Neverwinter carried across the breeze. However, things weren't quite right.  The hamlet was not whole.

The Brute Squad stirred from uneasy rest, each awakening amid the wreckage and weight of the previous day's discoveries, actions, and Sylvie's disappearance. Their leads were thin, their questions many.  While they weighed their next move, a knock halted their discussion.

 [[Primcross|Aldrich Sternfollow]], elder of Primcross had come to check on Leyawiin and the party. His demeanor was calm but resolute. He confirmed what the party already suspected: whatever had taken Sylvie was not ordinary, and chasing it blindly could cost more lives. He encouraged them to continue forward rather than linger, trusting that by following their own path, they might uncover Sylvie's. He assured Leyawiin the town would guard her shop and promised that if Sylvie returned, word would reach her. With that, the elder departed, leaving the party with both reassurance and a nudge to their next destination.

With little left to gain in Primcross, the party gathered themselves and set out toward Neverwinter.

The journey from the quiet hamlet into Neverwinter marked a stark shift. The city rose ahead in grand scale—towering walls, temple spires, banners of Lord Neverember snapping overhead, and a constant flow of merchants, nobles, sailors, and adventurers from every corner of the realm. A true melting pot.

At the gate, entry proved less than smooth. Guards, suspicious and blunt, halted the group. A poorly timed remark nearly escalated the situation before Sarhice intervened, redirecting the interaction with just enough tact. Invoking their connection to [[Boniface Abernathy]], they were allowed entry, though not without some rough handling and warnings from the guards.

Inside, the city overwhelmed. The sheer size, sound, and diversity of Neverwinter pushed the party slightly off balance. Streets teemed with life: illusion-performing bards, children at play, merchants shouting prices. Even experienced travelers felt the scale here.

The party was quickly split in the confusion, with the crowd density scattering them briefly before regrouping near the gate. Once aligned again, they followed the main thoroughfare south, using the flow of traffic to guide them deeper into the city.

Moving through Neverwinter, the architecture shifted into a more refined district. Clean stonework, well-maintained buildings, and ordered streets surrounding the Blue Lake.

It was here Bronn was approached unexpectedly.

A well-dressed older man named Edran recognized something in him—his resemblance to Bronn’s father. Their exchange revealed fragments of Bronn’s past. Edran had known both his parents and expressed genuine sorrow at their passing. In parting, he pressed two items into Bronn’s hand:

- A black obsidian ring marked with a crescent moon
- A note reading: _“In the absence of light is truth.”_

Further analysis confirmed the symbol belonged to [[Shar]], Mistress of the Night, Lady of Loss. No magic radiated from the ring, but its implication was clear: Bronn’s past held deeper connections than he realized.

Edran departed as suddenly as he appeared, leaving Bronn—and the party—with more questions than answers.

Following directions, the party eventually reached the dock district, where trade dominated every sight and sound. Exotic goods, strange creatures, and foreign cargo moved in constant motion.

At the heart of it stood the [[Boniface Abernathy|Luminous Meridian Trading Company]], an extravagant, ostentatious structure that left no doubt of its owner’s personality.

Inside, they were directed upstairs to meet [[Boniface Abernathy]].

Boniface greeted them with his usual energy.  He was loud, charismatic, and opportunistic. After brief pleasantries (and clear lack of recognition regarding Leyawiin prior to their meeting in Erentir or Sylvie), he shifted quickly to business.

He presented a contract:

- The party would travel to [[Chult]], specifically Port Nyanzaru
- Their mission: map, explore, and recover artifacts and knowledge
- All recovered items belong primarily to the company
- Payment included:
    - 75 gold per member (signing bonus)
    - 750 gold party provisioning fund
- Additional compensation tied loosely to discoveries
- High risk acknowledged; minimal liability for death

Despite clearly one-sided terms, the party negotiated modest improvements and ultimately agreed.

Boniface provided key details:

- Destination: Chult, an untamed jungle rich with ruins and danger
- Known threats include increased undead activity
- Guides are essential upon arrival
- The expedition is largely uncharted.  There is significant opportunity for both wealth and discovery

He assigned them passage aboard the Dawnmere, captained by Seryn Vael’Thalor, and instructed them to prepare before departure.


Following the contract signing, the party diverged again to handle personal matters.

**Bronn**  
Seeking guidance, Bronn traveled alone to the Neverdeath Graveyard, seat of Kelemvor’s influence in Neverwinter. There, among tombs and quiet sanctity, he prayed and recommitted himself to his faith. His devotion was acknowledged. He emerged strengthened, with renewed clarity and divine favor.

**Veldar (and partial group)**  
Returning to the Blue Lake District, Veldar located an enchanter’s shop: Charter & Charm, operated by Lysandra. He attempted to commission an enchantment to enhance his weapon.

- Base Offer: 200 gold
- Timeline: ~1 week
- Expedited (with additional enchanters): higher cost, ~2 days
- Refusal to travel with the party

Negotiations stalled. The party noted that Chult itself, being a major port, may offer better or faster magical services.

With contracts signed, gold distributed, and next steps forming, the Brute Squad ventures ever forward to their destiny: 

- A journey across the sea
- An expedition into Chult’s deadly jungles
- A growing web of personal mysteries (Sylvie, Bronn’s past, the Codex)

Their immediate priorities:

- Finalize provisions
- Secure any last preparations or enchantments
- Report to the harbor and board the Dawnmere

The path ahead is dangerous, but full of opportunity.
