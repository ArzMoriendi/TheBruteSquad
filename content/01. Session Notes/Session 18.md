# Previous Session Recap
Our last two sessions found our heroes deep beneath the ruined mining outpost of [[Hammerfall Run]], standing within a forgotten ritual chamber carved from ancient stone. There, amidst shattered goliath zombies and towering natural pillars, the Brute Squad confronted a [[Thayan mage]] attempting to corrupt a mysterious relic of stone and ruby pulsing with unnatural power. The battle nearly broke them. [[GhoulMan]], newly arrived through a planar rift and still reeling from the destruction of his homeland, was magically turned against the party. [[Veldar]] was frozen solid beneath a devastating Cone of Cold, and the mage herself revealed troubling knowledge of [[Zwargie]] and relics stolen long before. Yet through stubborn resolve, steel, spellcraft, and sheer will, the Brute Squad survived. The mage escaped into the darkness, but the chamber — and the strange artifact within it — belonged to the party.

That night, resting within the chamber itself, each member of the Brute Squad experienced visions, revelations, or transformations. Veldar again felt the primal presence of the Beast strengthening within him. Zwargie heard the approving whispers of the mysterious power guiding him. GhoulMan relived the slaughter of his wife and children at the hands of invading gnolls, awakening more determined and dangerous than before. Bronn rose spiritually enlightened beneath the quiet guidance of [[Kelemvor]], while [[Leyawiin]] alone awoke uncertain, realizing the path of the Bladesinger no longer felt like her own. When at last they left Hammerfall Run behind, the mountain itself seemed intent on claiming them. A brutal blizzard trapped them upon the slopes, and even after the storm broke, avalanche and falling stone battered the party during their descent toward [[Mirabar]].

Upon returning to the great mountain city, the Brute Squad was welcomed not as suspects, but as honored adventurers. Before Justiciar [[Geldryn Axebinder]], Durni Copperpick confirmed their deeds within Hammerfall Run, and Veldar was finally cleared of all suspicion. When the party presented the relic they had recovered, Geldryn revealed its true identity: the legendary Stoneheart Reliquary, a mythical dwarven artifact capable of transmuting common ore into its purest form. Understanding the danger and significance of the relic, the party entrusted it to Mirabar’s protection and were rewarded richly for their service — gold, a Bag of Holding, noble travel papers bearing the Justiciar’s seal, access to Mirabar’s libraries, and future use of the city’s portal chambers. In the libraries, Leyawiin found clarity under the stern instruction of the dwarven evoker [[Harbek Bronzewarden]], choosing at last to abandon Bladesinging and embrace the path of Evocation magic.

With their newfound status came smaller, stranger adventures throughout Mirabar itself. The party debated how best to profit from the decaying heads they had been carrying since their earlier exploits, eventually selling the head of [[Kragnoroth the Drunk]] through Mirabar’s shadowy black market contacts for a reduced bounty. Bronn and GhoulMan ventured into the dwarven undercity in search of dire rams and instead found Ironwool Stables, where Bronn attempted — unsuccessfully, though not without compassion — to heal the gravely ill mother of the stablekeeper Dagna. Moved by his efforts, she sold him an aging but spirited ram named Geezer and promised him a favor yet to be claimed. After final purchases, reforged equipment, and one last meeting with the Justiciar, the Brute Squad chose their next destination: [[Neverwinter]]. Armed with Geldryn’s letter of certification, the party traveled through a Harper portal alongside Geezer himself, emerging safely at a hidden Harper station just outside the great coastal city. And there, standing upon unfamiliar soil with roads, seas, and fresh dangers before them, our heroes prepared to take their next steps into a wider world.

#  Scene Setter

The Brute Squad emerged from the portal at the Harper's outpost, just beyond [[Neverwinter]], stepping out beneath an archway set into a quiet tower on a grassy rise. What lay before them was not the bustling city they expected—but a smaller, familiar place for one among them.

They had arrived in the hamlet of [[Primcross]], [[Leyawiin]]'s hometown.

At a glance, the village appeared as it always had; simple homes of timber and stone, light trade traffic, and the distant looming walls of Neverwinter on the horizon. As they ventured forth, something was clearly off to Leyawiin. As the party descended into town, a subtle tension hung in the air. The normal rhythms of life continued, but muted. Townsfolk passed with a gloom, conversations were hushed, smiles absent, and the people avoided eye contact.

A familiar face, Marta the tavern keep, directed Leyawiin to her own shop, urging her to see for herself what the news was. As the party approached [[Leyawiin|Leyawiin's Literary Labrynth]], the truth settled in. The once well-kept bookstore had been ransacked. The door was damaged, the interior dark and disordered, shelves overturned, books scattered, and signs of a violent struggle evident throughout the space. Initially cursing her sister, Leyawiin ran inside, panicked. 

But there was no blood. No body. And no Sylvie.

Inside, some of the squad searched for answers or clues, while some looked for a bathroom and others looked for a basement.  They quickly determined the destruction was not random. Evidence suggested a targeted confrontation. Impact marks, arcane residue, and displaced objects pointed to a fight, not looting. Despite the chaos, key items remained untouched, including a hidden tome entrusted to Leyawiin by her mentor Agatha.

Near the counter, the group uncovered a scrap of parchment, likely overlooked during the attack. The message read:

- _“The Lattice must be awoken._ _Find the Codex._ _Agatha had it._ _Bring her to me. -_ Signed "_X_" and an unfamiliar sigil

Comprehension dawned on them: Sylvie had been taken, not killed, and the attackers were searching for something. 

As Leyawiin continued to search for Sylvie, [[Sarhice Vavoca|Sarhice]] and [[Bronn the Humble|Bronn]] continued searching for the basement. 

The party brought this information to [[Primcross|Aldrich Sternfollow]], the hamlet’s alderman. He confirmed that the attack occurred roughly two weeks prior. Villagers had responded to the disturbance, but by the time they arrived, Sylvie was already gone. There were no tracks, no signs of escape, and nothing to follow. It was as if the attacker had vanished.

The sigil on the note provided the first real lead.

An elder identified it as belonging to the Githyanki, a planar warrior race not native to the Material Plane. Known as disciplined and dangerous raiders, they are capable of planar travel and often serve unknown or higher powers. Their presence in Primcross was highly abnormal. This news hinted at the possibility that Sylvie may no longer be on this plane. 

Meanwhile, outside the main council office, [[Veldar]] wandered to the nearby tavern.  Purchasing two large mugs of ale for a silver each, he chatted up the local tavern keep.  Heading back to his friend's, Veldar found himself in a brief but brutal confrontation with a cloaked assailant attempting to mug him. Despite being intoxicated and alone, Veldar held his ground and drove the attacker off after a violent exchange.  Using "Thumpy" to his advantage, Veldar proved he could hold his own against muggers and cut purses. He eventually found his way to his friend's, bloody, and a bit worse for the wear, but still victorious. 

Back at the shop, the party took time to regroup and investigate further. The damage remained concentrated on the lower level, suggesting Sylvie had not been killed, as no remains or significant bloodshed were found. Magical residue pointed toward combat spells. Bronn's magical searching turned up no additional magic or arcane residue.  He also inspected the premises, looking for evidence of "unplanned skylights," or holes where kidnappers may have entered or exited. No damage was discovered, no clues left behind. 

The hidden tome itself proved unsettling. Though unreadable by the party, it radiated layered magic: abjuration, conjuration, and transmutation. Combined with the note’s reference to a “codex,” the party began to suspect this book was the target of the attack.

Leyawiin revealed her original purpose for adventuring: to learn more about this very artifact and prevent it from falling into the wrong hands.

As the group debated next steps, two potential paths emerged:

- Investigate [[Neverwinter]] for information, contacts, or black market leads
- Travel toward the Neverwinter Woods, where prior connections to kobold tribes might provide additional insight

As night fell, the party settled in at the ruined shop. They secured what they could, cleaned portions of the space, and settled in uneasily. Veldar had passed out on the floor of the shop as soon as he entered. Leyawiin withdrew to her sister’s room, overcome by the weight of the situation. [[Zwargie|Zwarg]] tucked in the shop privy.  [[Bronn the Humble|Bronn]] lay curled up in the fur of his mount, Geezer, in the back garden, while [[Sarhice Vavoca|Sarhice]] (borrowing Leyawiin's garden shovel), continued to dig through the night looking for the basement.  [[Ghoulman]] found a corner and curled up, sleeping restlessly. 

Where will the Brute Squad go next?
