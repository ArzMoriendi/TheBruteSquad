# Previous Session Recap

Following their successful negotiations with Boniface Abernathy and the signing of their expedition contract, the Brute Squad’s planned voyage to Chult was upended by prophetic visions, unexpected departures, and a sudden shift in priorities. While resting at the Mariner’s Rest, Zwarg experienced a transformative vision guided by an armored champion of justice and quietly departed the party, leaving behind his Gauntlets of Ogre Power to follow a new path.

Inspired by a deep-seated instinct to find her abducted sister Sylvie, Leyawiin resolved to abandon the Chult expedition, a sentiment echoed by Ghoulman—who sought vengeance for his ruined homeland—as well as Veldar and Sarhice. Amidst this morning turmoil, the group encountered a chaotic, milk-drinking, rope-tangled Tabaxi monk named Duck Behind the Willow, affectionately known as Ducky. Despite her eccentric demeanor, Ducky’s insider knowledge and ties to information networks directed the party toward the Harpers as their best lead for tracking down Sylvie.

While the rest of the party redirected their efforts, Bronn remained resolute in his commitment to the expedition, parting ways with his companions to board the _Dawnmere_ for Chult. The remaining members returned their extra gear—during which Sarhice received a cryptic tarot card reading from a mysterious child—and journeyed back toward Primcross. Arriving at the Harper waypoint near their original point of entry, the Brute Squad unified under a renewed purpose to uncover the truth and rescue Sylvie

#  Scene Setter

 The Brute Squad’s search for answers regarding Sylvie and extraplanar forces brought them back to the Harper safe house portal. Consulting with the outpost guard, the party used a magic mirror to speak directly with regional coordinator Brightcandle Resmythe Winphala. Winphala informed them that their requested specialist, Felcryn Flukereach, was currently stationed at an active archaeological dig site and would be unavailable for the next six months. She recommended finding a tinkerer or trap expert in Neverwinter’s Blacklake District, noted that a transport was leaving later in the day, and mentioned a Githyanki expert in extraplanar affairs.

Following Winphala's advice, the party ventured into Neverwinter’s Blacklake District to locate the *Smoldering Sprocket*, a workshop announced immediately by an explosion blowing out a window. Inside, they met Gogz, an eccentric goblin tinkerer and former Cragmaw Tribe member who lost his right arm and leg in a black-powder accident and replaced them with self-designed mechanical prosthetics. After a chaotic introduction involving Ducky inspecting shiny shop items and Gogz accidentally nearly firing his spring-loaded crossbow while boasting about his inventions, the shop master yelled down for Gogz to dismiss the customers. Prompted by the shouting, Gogz hastily agreed to join the party. Before leaving Neverwinter, Gogz stopped at a black powder provider and purchased 2 lbs of black powder for 35 gp.

The party made their way to Primcross to catch their transport—a griffin-pulled carriage—heading toward the dig site near Mount Hotenow. While the driver remained tightlipped throughout the journey, Gogz passed the time sharing stories of his upbringing with the Cragmaw Goblins. Upon arriving at the camp, the party searched the premises; finding minimal supplies of note, Gogz discovered key documents inside the leader's tent and fashioned improvised wax bombs using candle wax and fuses.

Descending into the ancient spire, the Brute Squad confronted a gauntlet of mechanical and magical hazards:

The hallway was lined with dart and arrow holes and littered with corpses. Hearing whirring and mechanical sounds, Gogz summoned his battle-boar simulacrum, Kevin, to test the floor. Determining the plates were currently non-operational, the party followed safely in the boar's footsteps.

Passing a sliced corpse, they navigated a swinging blade trap that also remained mysteriously inactive.

A scorched section humming with electric current led to a master lever. Gogz rode Kevin across to pull it, which jarred loose a torn piece of parchment reading: _"To whoever follows: don't reset the trap."_ After briefly toggling the traps on and off again, Gogz attempted to pry up a floor plate, but even with assistance, it remained firmly stuck.\

Deeper still, the party emerged into a narrow space suspended hundreds of feet over a yawning chasm. Spanning the gap were 12 disc-like stone platforms staggered like zipper teeth, acting as giant spinning tiddlywinks.

Kevin tested the first platform, which immediately flipped like a spinning coin, prompting Gogz to drop to his knees crying _"Keviiiiinn!"_ before quickly resummoning his boar.

Utilizing a successful Acrobatics check, Ducky sprinted across with a rope, dragging Leyawiin safely behind her.  Once the platforms stabilized, Sarhice, Gogz, Ghoulman, and Veldar successfully balanced their way across the chasm.

Reaching the inner chamber, the party encountered the rock gnome archaeologist Felcryn Flukereach, three human assistants, and one desiccated corpse. Felcryn warned them away from a glowing barrier sealing off a wall of black basalt stone, pointing to the lifeless assistant as proof of its lethality.

Examining the Sylvan poem inscribed on the wall, the party translated its riddle: only vessels empty of life's warm spark could cross safely. Gogz sent Kevin through the barrier with zero issues, while a foolish human assistant who touched it instantly withered away into dust.  

Studying a second Sylvan inscription on the basalt wall—translated through a rigorous effort—they learned the seal required a strike of raw, dark will. Stepping forward, Sarhice crossed the barrier unharmed, drew his blade, infused it with magical power, and struck the basalt wall. The barrier vanished, and the heavy stone doors swung open to reveal the passage beyond.
