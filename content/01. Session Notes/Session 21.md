# Previous Session Recap

Previously on The Brute Squad...

As the Brute Squad prepared to depart [[Neverwinter]] for [[Chult]], they spent their final day gathering supplies and making arrangements for the expedition. The party visited the harbor and learned that Captain [[Neverwinter|Seryn Vael'Thalor]] would not begin boarding the _Dawnmere_ until the following morning. While the group shopped for jungle gear and debated survival strategies, [[Zwargie|Zwarg]] became determined to find the ship himself. After discovering it was anchored offshore, he attempted to swim out to it and promptly required rescue by local fishermen. Meanwhile, the rest of the party acquired hammocks, mosquito netting, climbing gear, waterproof cloaks, and other supplies necessary for surviving the dangers of Chult.

Preparations continued throughout Neverwinter as the party sought additional information about their destination. Merchants and cartographers reinforced the idea that Chult remained largely unmapped and that local guides would be essential once they reached [[Chult|Port Nyanzaru]]. [[Bronn the Humble|Bronn]] purchased a cask of fine wine and arranged for it to be delivered to the _Dawnmere_, while [[Veldar]]ar purchased a compass that he hoped might somehow aid him in finding his lost tribe. Despite their efforts, the party found few concrete answers about the jungle awaiting them and instead resolved to rely on their own skills and whatever allies they could find upon arrival.

That evening, after settling into the *Mariner's Rest*, [[GhoulMan]] experienced a powerful supernatural vision. Within a cosmic arena overseen by celestial and infernal judges, he faced a series of increasingly difficult trials. He defeated two Gnoll Pack Lords and was rewarded with a *Greatsword of Sharpness*, overcame two Gladiators and received two *Rings of Demonic Protection*, and finally slew a mighty Minotaur Champion. Through these trials, GhoulMan proved that true strength lies not in never falling, but in always rising again. As dawn arrived, the Brute Squad completed their preparations and stood ready to leave the [[Faerûn|Sword Coast]] behind and begin their long-awaited voyage to Chult.

#  Scene Setter

Following the party's successful negotiations with [[Boniface Abernathy]] and the signing of their expedition contract with the *Luminous Meridian Trading Company*, the group retired for the evening at the *Mariner's Rest*. What appeared to be a routine night before embarking for [[Chult]] instead became a turning point for the campaign, marked by prophetic dreams, unexpected departures, a strange new ally, and a complete shift in direction. 

While the others slept, [[Zwargie|Zwarg]] experienced his own vision-like dream.

The familiar whispers from the mysterious statuette he carried gave way to something entirely different. The darkness of sleep faded into the warm light of dawn as he found himself standing atop a gentle hillside overlooking a peaceful valley. Crystal streams wound through fields of wildflowers. Ancient oaks stood watch over farmsteads and cottages where families began their morning routines. Children laughed in the fields and elders rested on front porches. It was a vision of a world untouched by fear, violence, or suffering.

Standing nearby was an armored figure, strange but familiar.  The warrior appeared entirely human, clad in radiant plate armor that seemed to shine with the light of the rising sun itself. At his side rested a magnificent glaive. His demeanor carried absolute calm and unwavering certainty.

The figure spoke, and his voice was strong, clear, and familiar.  The voice from the statuette.

> "Walk with me."

Zwarg followed.

As they crossed the valley, the figure explained that peace such as this did not happen by chance. Safe places existed only because someone was willing to stand between the innocent and those who would harm them.  Their conversation was interrupted by the sight of a terrified half-orc family fleeing along a road. Pursuing them were three armed brigands, dragging a young girl with them while tormenting the family. Without hesitation, the armored warrior stepped into their path.

The brigands were given an opportunity to stop. They refused.

The warrior raised his glaive, and with effortless certainty cut down the leader in a single stroke. The remaining brigands immediately begged for mercy. His response was simple.

> "When she begged, did you listen?"

Justice was swift, and the other brigands were also cut down.  The warrior then turned to Zwarg and imparted the following thought: 

> "Mercy is sacred. It cannot exist without justice. To spare the unrepentant at the cost of the innocent is not compassion. It is surrender."

As dawn crested fully over the valley, the warrior offered Zwarg a final choice.

> "Every dawn is another chance to choose the light. Will you walk with me again?"

Zwarg accepted. When he awoke, he understood that his path no longer lay with the party.  Without waking anyone, without a farewell, and without fanfare, Zwarg chose to leave. He gathered his belongings, leaving behind his Gauntlets of Ogre Power as his only goodbye, and departed into the world, following the call of the unnamed figure from his vision whose influence had guided him throughout his journey.

By the time the party awoke, he was already gone. 

While Zwarg received purpose, [[Leyawiin]] received clarity.

Despite recently committing to Boniface Abernathy's Chultan expedition, she awoke deeply unsettled. Thoughts of her missing sister, [[Primcross|Sylvie]], weighed heavily upon her. Though she still lacked concrete leads, she became increasingly convinced that boarding the *Dawnmere* and sailing to Chult was the wrong decision.  Something within her insisted that Sylvie's trail lay elsewhere. For perhaps the first time since arriving in [[Neverwinter]], Leyawiin chose to trust that instinct and search for her sister rather than follow the commitment to explore unknown lands. 

As the group gathered for breakfast in the *Mariner's Rest*, still unaware that Zwarg had departed, they began to discuss the next steps of their adventure.  Leyawiin stated her intention to find her sister rather than travel to Chult. [[GhoulMan]] imparted the details of his vision and stated that he wanted to avenge his family and his village and find the people behind the massacre.  [[Veldar]] and [[Sarhice Vavoca|Sarhice]] also commented they were unsure about traveling to Chult.  At this point, Leyawiin realized Zwarg was not there, and the party quickly searched for him.  Leyawiin found his gauntlets and deduced he left the party for his own path.  Quickly attuning to them, she took out her aggression on a nearby chair.  The party ultimately settled into their breakfast, agitated, befuddled, and altogether grumpy. 

It was during this morning meal that they encountered one of the strangest individuals they had met thus far.

A small hooded figure quietly entered the tavern and slipped into a corner without introducing themselves.   The tavern staff provided her milk, which she consumed with enthusiasm and considerably less grace.  Spilled milk, scattered supplies, and general confusion followed in her wake.

Distracted by the loud slurping and jumble of net and rope, the party took an interest in the stranger and chose to introduce themselves.  This figure revealed themselves to be Duck Behind the Willow, a Tabaxi monk who quickly became known simply as Ducky. 

Ducky appeared nervous, skittish, and perpetually on the verge of becoming tangled in something. Throughout the morning she managed to get herself caught in ropes, freed from ropes, and then somehow entangled in ropes again. Even simple tasks often devolved into minor disasters.  Additionally, several bottles of an unknown (but questionable) substance crashed to floor, leaving a mess for the party to clean up.  Ducky disavowed knowledge of the substance, stating they were there to begin with. 

The party attempted to learn more about their new acquaintance, only to receive a collection of increasingly bizarre stories about her life and recent travels, including a tale about a bed that turned into a dragon, then back into a bed. At various points Ducky seemed completely sincere while recounting explanations that bordered on the absurd. Despite every reason to doubt her, the group found themselves inexplicably accepting much of what she said.  Whether through genuine innocence, natural charisma, or simply being too strange to question, Ducky somehow convinced the party that her stories were entirely believable.

Ducky's unusual mannerisms continued throughout the session.  From frequently becoming distracted by random objects, to showing an extremely strong interest in ropes and knots, or displaying curious feline behaviors and becoming immediately interested whenever rats or rodents were mentioned, Ducky created chaos almost entirely by accident. 

Behind the eccentricity, however, there was evidence of experience and insight.  When discussion inevitably turned back toward Sylvie and the lack of viable leads, Ducky's knowledge of monastic traditions and information networks that the rest of the party lacked helped prod the party to the best, closest option available: a trip to the Harpers. As a widespread organization dedicated to protecting the innocent, preserving knowledge, and gathering intelligence, they represented one of the few groups capable of helping the party uncover information about Sylvie's disappearance.

While the rest of the party reconsidered their plans, [[Bronn the Humble|Bronn]] remained committed to the expedition.  When the belltowers tolled out the time, he said his farewells, and concluded his business in Neverwinter. Gathering his supplies, he mounted Geezer and headed toward the docks.  The party watched him depart, a bit of green skin from his "orc-nugget" pouch visible from his belt, knowing he intended to continue toward Chult aboard the *Dawnmere*.

Though their paths now diverged, Bronn's story remains unfinished and the possibility of running into their companion again remains. 

With Ducky's suggestion in mind, the party set out toward [[Primcross]] and the Harper waypoint located near the portal through which they had originally arrived. Along the way they discussed the Harpers, their reputation throughout [[Faerûn]], and the possibility that the organization might possess information unavailable anywhere else. 

As they traveled through the various equipment shops and other merchant districts returning their unnecessary equipment, a young fortune teller approached Sarhice and offered to read his cards.

Sarhice drew:

- The Skull - past hardship and suffering.
- The World - change, growth, and many possible futures.
- The Sun - hope, guidance, and a brighter future.

Sarhice silently pondered the meaning, handing the waiting child a bit of copper. The child accepted the tip and vanished back into the crowds of Neverwinter. 

Leaving Neverwinter behind, the party traveled back toward the Harper waypoint near the portal from which they had first entered the region. Following Ducky's guidance, the group arrived at the Harper station with a renewed sense of purpose. Find the Harpers. Find information. Find Sylvie.  The session concluded as the party reached the station and prepared to begin what would become the next major phase of the campaign!
